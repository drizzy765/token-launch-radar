How We Value

Model v3
Valuation Identity
TVL × Multiple × Growth × Age × Chains
1

Category Multiple

Median Market Cap ÷ TVL of live peers in the same category, Winsorized at P10–P90. A category median requires at least 6 trading comps. Below 6, the global market median is used to prevent thin-sample distortions.

2

TVL Growth Modifier

Momentum factor: 1 + 0.006 × Δ7d%, clamped between -20% and +35% (+/-12% to +21% adjustment). Missing historical change defaults to 1.000.

3

Protocol Seasoning

Asymptotic maturity curve: 0.68 + 0.42 × (1 - e-t/1.5), where t is operational years from listing date. Unreported listing dates receive a baseline 0.750 factor.

4

Multichain Breadth

Cross-network distribution factor: 1 + 0.03 × extra_chains, capped at 12 extra chains (+36% maximum expansion bonus).

5

Confidence Tiers

  • High ≥ 12 category peers anchor multiple
  • Fair 6 to 11 category peers anchor multiple
  • Thin 1 to 5 peers; global median fallback
  • Unanchored 0 peers; global median fallback
