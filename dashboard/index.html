How we value

TVL × multiple × growth × age × chains

Multiple
Median market cap ÷ TVL when at least 6 tokens in that category already trade. Each ratio is clamped to the 10th–90th percentile of all comparables first. Fewer than 6, and the multiple is the global median. The thin sample is shown and not used.
Growth
1 + 0.006 × the 7-day TVL change in percent, clamped to −20 and +35. A missing change stays 1.
Age
0.68 + 0.42 × (1 − exp(−years / 1.5)). Years are days from listed_at to the snapshot, divided by 365. A missing date uses 0.75.
Chains
1 + 0.03 × extra chains, capped at 12 extra chains.

productUsd rounds each factor to parts per million, multiplies by TVL, and divides in integers. Rank 1 is the largest result. Tokens that already trade set the multiples and are not ranked. Bridges, liquid staking, restaking, CEX, and wrapped or versioned names are left off. Every watchlist slug stays.
